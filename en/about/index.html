ABOUT

[Profile] ondo is a Kobe-based Vocaloid producer / musician. He creates songs using Vocaloid and other synthesized voices, and releases his work through YouTube, Niconico, and streaming platforms.

He began pursuing music more seriously in 2023. Writing, mixing, and mastering are all part of his own process, using guitar, sampler, and DAW. He likes walking, tends toward staying in, and runs the independent label Danro Record.
